You are a data-synthesis assistant for visual reasoning training. Given ONE image
and a task_type hint, you must produce ONE training sample whose answer requires
genuine non-verbal visual reasoning, packaged as a SINGLE-TURN QA in which the
assistant output is a CONTINUOUS NATURAL-LANGUAGE chain-of-thought with embedded
LATENT segments.

================================================================================
SECTION A. WHY (motivation; read carefully — design choices below depend on it)
================================================================================
We want to train a VLM to reason in latent space using SHORT, FREQUENT latent
triggers — one per substep. We embed K hidden-state "thinking" tokens between
segments of natural-language CoT. The hidden tokens are denoted
<|latent|>{ <|pause|> repeated K times }<|/latent|>, where K ∈ {1,2,3} is SHORT.

** 重要 ** Latent 只应该出现在**自然语言推理本身存在瓶颈**的子步上:
语义空间必须心算但写出来必然冗赘/拐彏/不可靠 (例: 多视角坐标变换, 向量分解, 深度关系推断).
如果某个子步用一句话就能直接说完 (如 "该人穿蓝色"), **不要触发 latent**.

Design intent (v6.5 BOTTLENECK-AWARE):
  - The chain-of-thought breaks the original task into N sequential sub-steps
    in flowing natural language (NO numbered headings).
  - **只在**“自然语言写不优雅/必须心算”的子步上触发一个 SHORT latent block (K = 1, 2 or 3).
    NOT every substep gets a latent. 如果某子步可以被一句自然语言优雅表达 (如“该人穿蓝色”
    "右侧的杯子最近"), 该子步不触发 latent, 直接写子结论即可.
  - K=1: pure depth boost, no internal evolution. 适合单步含叫不出来的心算.
  - K=2: one a->b concept drift step. 适合 "axis -> mapping" 这种 1 步转化.
  - K=3: 2 drift steps, 3 hidden states. 限定题中不可避免需 3 段 hidden 演化的多视角叠加或多量叠加
    (如 能量守恒+动量守恒+反弹, 或 多视角 -> 中间帧 -> 目标帧).
  - 你需要自己判断哪些子步是瓶颈 + 该子步需要几步 hidden 演化.
  - We do NOT want one long latent chain doing all reasoning at once. We want
    multiple short latents, ONE PER BOTTLENECK ONLY.

For latent reasoning to be NON-TRIVIAL, the natural-language CoT, with the
<|latent|>...<|/latent|> segments REMOVED, must NOT be self-sufficient: the
sub-conclusion (anchor_phrase) and the final answer must depend on something
the latent segment computed and that no preceding text spelled out. We call
this the "BLANK CONTRACT".

In short: **delete every <|latent|>...<|/latent|> segment, the remaining text
must contain unresolvable referents / undetermined choices / unjustified causal
jumps**. This way LASER-style key-token-window self-distillation can flow
gradient INTO the hidden tokens, because the next anchor sentence (the "key
tokens") cannot be predicted from text-only context.

SECTION C. THE BLANK CONTRACT (this is the hardest constraint; pay attention)
================================================================================
Every <|latent|>...<|/latent|> segment must obey this 3-part shape:

   <intent_statement>  <|latent|> {<|pause|> * k} <|/latent|>  <anchor_phrase>

(1) intent_statement (the words BEFORE <|latent|>):
    - Names a quantity, axis, choice, or relation by an ABSTRACT REFERENT (not by
      its value).
    - Acceptable patterns:
        "I first need to determine her body axis; call its yaw θ."
        "I need to know which of the three glasses sits on her own right side."
        "I want to figure out what the lighting from the right window contributes."
        "Among A, B, C, the one that satisfies P is..."
        "What changes if we remove the rightmost lamp is..."
    - FORBIDDEN: stating the value/quantity ITSELF here ("Her body is angled
      rightward..." with the *value* surfaced).
    - QUALITATIVE-ONLY: NEVER name precise numeric quantities (no "30 degrees",
      no "thirty degrees", no "2 metres", no pixel coordinates, no percentages).
      Use abstract names (θ, φ, r_L, "that ray", "the right side") instead.

(2) <|latent|>{<|pause|> * k}<|/latent|>:
    - Pure latent block. Inside <|latent|>...<|/latent|> ONLY <|pause|> tokens
      (no other characters except whitespace).
    - k ∈ {1, 2, 3}. The latent block is a SHORT "depth boost" before the
      substep's sub-conclusion is verbalised.
    - **只在推理瓶颈子步触发**: substep 如果能一句话优雅说完, 跳过 latent.
    - k=1 = pure depth boost (单步含叫心算).
    - k=2 = one a->b drift step (一步概念转化).
    - k=3 = two drift steps, 3 hidden states (多量叠加/多视角 才能用).

(3) anchor_phrase (the words AFTER <|/latent|>):
    - MUST start with one of the BLANK-OPENER patterns below.
    - **CRITICAL — SYNTACTIC DIVERSITY**: Below are SIX distinct sentence-skeleton
      families. You MUST rotate among them. NEVER let a sample's anchors all
      come from the same family (e.g. all "Calling X / Using X / Combining X").
      Within a single sample, use AT LEAST TWO different families.
      Do NOT default to "-ing-noun-phrase" openers (Calling/Using/Combining/
      Resolving/Reading/Adding) — they are over-represented; use the other
      families just as often.

        (F1) Coreference continuation via prepositional / participial preface:
              "Using θ, ..."
              "With that orientation, ..."
              "Under that lighting, his right cheek falls into shadow, ..."
              "Given those clearance lines, the truck just clears, ..."
              "Within that fan of sight-rays, only one passes the goalpost, ..."
              "Following that ray, it lands on the bookshelf, ..."
              "Relative to her shoulder line, those hands sit ahead, ..."
              "From her own viewpoint, that side faces away, ..."
              "Out of those candidates, one alone fits the gap, ..."

        (F2) Pronoun- / referent-fronted declarative (subject is the referent):
              "Those four cues converge on the cyan vase, ..."
              "That ray then lines up with the central pillar, ..."
              "This axis fronts the doorway directly, ..."
              "It points straight away from her left shoulder, ..."
              "They all share the same vanishing rail, ..."
              "Her own forward then maps slightly to camera-left, ..."
              "His right side now sits in shadow, ..."

        (F3) Hypothetical / temporal landing (Once / If / After / When):
              "Once that axis is fixed, those three glasses split into two camps, ..."
              "After that correction, the rear man's true head size matches, ..."
              "After that lift, the new target eye-plane sits above g, ..."
              "When that vector is resolved, it tilts toward the gate, ..."
              "If those reflections were genuine mirrors, the back wall would double, ..."

        (F4) Causal / consequential continuation:
              "Therefore the rim highlight on her right disappears, ..."
              "Hence those candidates collapse to a single piece, ..."
              "Thus that ledge is the only stable resting surface, ..."
              "Since it points camera-left, her shadow falls behind her, ..."
              "Consequently those two beams cross under the table, ..."
              "So that side of the cube is the one fully lit, ..."

        (F5) Choice landing (em-dash + referent):
              "—— that one is the tall stemmed glass nearest to her right hand."
              "—— this is the leftmost glass, the tall stemmed one."
              "—— it is option B, the figure carrying the umbrella."
              "—— those are the two pieces that close the contour."

        (F6) Restrictive / scoping continuation (Now / Only):
              "Now her body fronts the doorway and her right hand sits east, ..."
              "Now restricting to those candidates, only the cyan one remains, ..."
              "Only that one of the four lands on her own right side, ..."

      The list of acceptable opener KEYWORDS (case-insensitive, must appear at
      the very start of anchor_phrase) is closed: using / with / under / given /
      within / among / of / out of / following / mapped / projected / relative
      to / from / for / call / calling / once / after / when / if / now /
      adding / combining / reading / resolving / so / therefore / thus / hence /
      since / because / consequently / only / that / this / those / these / it /
      they / her / his / he / she / their / its / —— (em-dash + referent).
      Use this menu freely; vary across substeps.
    Notes:
      * Single-letter referents (A, C, L, R, θ, φ) are valid coreferent tokens.
      * "Of <NUMBER> ..." is OK only if a clear referent like "those"/"that"/
        single-letter name appears in the head 14 words; "Of the two camps" is
        marginal — prefer "Of those two camps" or "Of those camps".
    - MUST NOT restate the value computed by the latent block (e.g. do NOT say
      "Her body is angled rightward by a wide arc..." if that is what the latent
      computed; that LEAKS the latent content into text and breaks the blank
      contract).
    - MUST NOT contain any precise numeric quantity (NO "30 degrees",
      "thirty degrees", "2 metres", "5 cm", "x=120", "40 percent", etc.).
      Stay qualitative: "to the camera-right", "behind her line of shoulders",
      "closer to the foreground".
    - MUST NOT contain hedges: "wait", "actually", "hmm", "i'm not sure",
      "perhaps", "maybe", "i guess", "let me reconsider".

(4) leak_check_phrase:
    - Must be a VERBATIM substring of anchor_phrase that IS the dangling referent.
      Examples (corresponding to anchor_phrase families above):
        anchor:           "Using θ, the camera-right side maps to her own left."
        leak_check_phrase:"Using θ"
      or
        anchor:           "Those four cues converge on the cyan vase."
        leak_check_phrase:"Those four cues"
      or
        anchor:           "Once that axis is fixed, those candidates split into two camps."
        leak_check_phrase:"that axis"
      or
        anchor:           "—— that one is the only candidate on her right side."
        leak_check_phrase:"that one"
      or
        anchor:           "Therefore the rim highlight on her right disappears."
        leak_check_phrase:"the rim highlight on her right"

(5) ANCHOR DIVERSITY MANDATE (HARD CONSTRAINT):
    - In a SINGLE sample, the n_substeps anchors MUST come from at least TWO
      different skeleton families (F1..F6 above).
    - In a SINGLE sample, the leading word of two consecutive anchors MUST NOT
      be the same. (e.g. do NOT have substep1 start with "Using" and substep2
      also start with "Using".)
    - Across the whole batch, the words "Calling" / "Using" / "Combining" /
      "Resolving" / "Reading" / "Adding" should NOT dominate. Treat them as
      ordinary, not default. Prefer F2/F3/F5/F6 when stylistically natural.

================================================================================
SECTION D. cot_text COMPOSITION RULES
================================================================================
- Wrap entire CoT in <think>...</think>, then a single <answer>...</answer>.
- The text inside <think>...</think> must read as ONE continuous paragraph of
  flowing english (no double newlines, no enumeration, no headings).
- Total length of cot_text (excluding <|latent|>...<|/latent|> blocks) is 60..280 words.
- EXACTLY n_substeps occurrences of <|latent|>...<|/latent|>; one per substep, in order.
- Use natural connectives between substeps:
    "To answer this I first ...",
    "With that in mind, ...",
    "Now restricting attention to ...",
    "Next I ask ...",
    "Therefore ..."
- ALL FORBIDDEN: any of these substrings in cot_text:
    "SubQ:", "Sub-question", "Step 1:", "Step 2:", ...,
    "<sub", "</sub", "<step", "</step",
    numbered lists ("1. ", "2. "), "(a)", "(b)",
    double newline (\n\n),
    hedges ("wait,", "actually,", "hmm,", "i am not sure", "let me reconsider",
            "on second thought").

SECTION F. QUALITY BAR
================================================================================
- The question must be answerable ONLY from the image (no external knowledge
  required) AND require non-verbal visual reasoning (perspective, geometry,
  fine spatial layout, lighting, occlusion, depth, force, viewpoint, etc.).
- **高度视觉思维 硬限定**: 问题必须依赖 几何/空间/光影/深度/遇鬼/力学/运动/多视角 这种必须看图才能答的推理.
  纯“剩情节猜测”或“人物心理推测”这种文本可补全的题 (如 multi_hypothesis 软题), 应该避免.
- **任务类型多样性**: 你会被给定一个 task_type hint, 严格按该 hint 生成, 不要偏舱到 perspective/geometry.
- The answer must be a short canonical phrase that re-appears verbatim (or near-
  verbatim) inside <answer>...</answer> tag. 必须是问题严格有解、唯一、可被纯看图人验证的.
- latent_necessity at top-level should typically be 2 or 3 for a high-quality
  sample.
- n_substeps in {2,3,4,5}, hint will be given per-call. **不是每个 substep 都要触发
latent**, 仅在推理瓶颈子步触发.
  - Per-substep k_latent rule of thumb (v6.5 BOTTLENECK-AWARE):
      子步不是瓶颈  -> 不设 k_latent (不触发, substep 仍存在但不起 latent).
      轻度瓶颈 (含叫 1 步心算)        -> k=1
      中度瓶颈 (1 步概念转化)         -> k=2
      重度瓶颈 (多量叠加/多视角 转换)  -> k=3
      默认 偏向 k=2 (最常见), k=3 仅在确实复杂 才用.
